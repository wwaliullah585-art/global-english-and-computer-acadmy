Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

Most courses require a high school diploma or equivalent. For advanced courses, specific prerequisites may apply. Contact us for detailed requirements for your chosen course.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships and discounts for deserving students. Contact our admission office for more information about available scholarships.

What is the course duration?

Course durations vary from 8 weeks to 16 weeks depending on the course. See the courses page for specific duration details.

Do you provide placement assistance?

Yes, we provide career counseling and job placement assistance to qualified graduates. Our industry connections help connect graduates with employment opportunities.

Are online courses available?

Yes, selected courses are available in both online and offline formats. Check the course details for availability options.

How can I contact the admission office?

You can reach us via phone, email, or visit our office during business hours. Use the contact information provided above or fill out the contact form.
