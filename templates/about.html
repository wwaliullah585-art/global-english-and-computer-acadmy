About GECA

Global English and Computer Academy - Bannu

Welcome to GECA

Global English and Computer Academy (GECA) is a premier educational institution located in Bannu, dedicated to providing comprehensive, industry-aligned education in computer science, IT, and English language proficiency.

Since our establishment, we have been committed to nurturing talent, fostering innovation, and preparing students for successful careers in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.

Our Mission

To provide quality, accessible, and affordable education that empowers students to become skilled professionals, innovative thinkers, and responsible global citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution recognized for excellence, innovation, and the success of our graduates. We aspire to create an ecosystem of continuous learning and professional development.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Student-Centric Approach, Continuous Improvement, and Social Responsibility guide everything we do.
